Weigh-in is an input, not a readout

Tap the number, type, done — the morning weigh-in is a daily habit and shouldn't cost a screen. What you read is the 7-day average, because daily weight is water and salt. Your own spreadsheet had that column; it just didn't make it into round one.

Ask sits at the bottom, pre-loaded with context

It already knows today is Pull, which five lifts are on, and what your targets are. No re-explaining. The placeholder is a real sentence rather than "Ask anything", because a concrete example teaches what it's for.

"1 of 4" replaced volume

Both are counts, but only one changes what you do next. Behind on sessions is actionable; 120,285 lbs is trivia.

Sage still appears exactly once

Today's session owns it. The Ask button is a dashed outline with a Mist dot deliberately — present, clearly interactive, not competing for the tap.

Body fat is absent on purpose

You flagged it as a later phase and that's right: photo estimates carry roughly ±3–4% error, wider than a month's real change. It would read as noise, not progress — so it waits for a method that can actually measure it.

The status banner states a judgement, not a number

"On track" versus "two sessions at risk" — computed from sessions remaining against days left and the time each needs. A progress bar would make you do that arithmetic yourself.

"Change what's left" is a narrow model call

It rewrites only the remaining days, not the whole week. Cheaper, faster, and it can't accidentally undo sessions you've already logged.

Closing the week names the consequence

Not "are you sure?" but which sessions get marked missed and what that costs — they stop counting toward progression. The schema already carries a superseded state for the outgoing plan.

"Plan next week" is a ghost button

Mid-week, adjusting is the likely action and planning ahead is the rare one. The weights flip on your check-in day.

Three questions

01

What's in Review?

The one tab we haven't discussed. My instinct: per-lift strength curves, a personal records list, and body-weight trend — the "am I actually getting stronger" view, on a month-plus horizon. Nothing weekly, since Plan owns that.

02

Does a missed session really not count?

Closing the week marks unlogged sessions missed and excludes them from progression. The alternative is carrying them forward. Excluding is simpler and honest, but it means a travel week shows as a gap forever.

03

Rename the app?

Repo, titles, and metadata still say "Personal Trainer". You've settled on Saturday Fitness — confirm and I'll change it everywhere while it's four files, not forty.
